What to expect when you contact David

Start with the person and the property

Use the same number shown on this site, (425) 548-1993, or the property form. Tell David why you are considering a sale, who owns the house, and how to reach you. You do not need a finished repair estimate to begin.

Build a shared picture

Discuss occupancy, known condition, access, documents already available, and the dates that matter. David may need more information or a property visit before deciding whether to prepare a purchase offer.

Read the offer as a decision

Confirm the purchasing entity, price, deposit, review conditions, who pays each cost, and closing and possession dates. Ask about any term you do not understand and compare alternatives. A conversation creates no obligation.

Prepare your next decision

Make a practical agenda for David and a separate checklist for your own decision. Mark only the facts you have confirmed. Use the notes to record questions, then print or save your page as a PDF.
