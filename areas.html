What helps confirm property fit

  • Send the street address or parcel number if the property has an unusual address.
  • Explain who occupies the home and who can authorize access. Remote owners can begin by sharing information they already have.
  • For island or rural properties, mention ferry access, well or septic systems, outbuildings and practical access restrictions.
  • For a property outside the six counties, confirm whether it can be considered before spending time preparing an offer package. No broader service footprint is represented here.
