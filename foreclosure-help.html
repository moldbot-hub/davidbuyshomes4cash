Talking with David when a mortgage deadline is involved

A buyer conversation is one possible sale discussion. It does not pause a foreclosure, negotiate your loan, or confirm that you have enough time to sell.

Independent homeowner help

The Washington Department of Commerce directs homeowners to free housing counseling at 1-877-894-4663. Read the official Foreclosure Fairness Program information. Seek qualified legal advice about your circumstances. This website is a property buyer, not a counselor, law firm or government program.

Keep these questions separate

Keep independent help moving

Contact your servicer and a qualified housing counselor promptly. Share the actual notice and deadline with your own adviser. Do not wait for David to review a property before getting help.

Tell David which date is real

Say whether you have received a notice and what date it lists. Do not send account numbers or full loan documents through an initial marketing inquiry. Ask what information a buyer and title company would need through a suitable channel.

Separate a proposal from a solution

Ask about unresolved title, payoff, signing and funding conditions. Until the relevant professionals confirm the transaction and applicable requirements, a proposed sale is not assurance that a scheduled sale will be avoided.
