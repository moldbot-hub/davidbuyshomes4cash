Questions and answers

Is David Buys Homes 4 Cash a separate buyer from Washington Home Solutions?

This website is operated by Washington Home Solutions LLC. David is the contact for that operating buyer. Related websites are not separate competing buyers or separate city offices.

What should I ask on the first call?

Ask who will buy the property, what information is still needed, whether an inspection or visit is required, and when you can expect the next update. Explain your priorities and any deadline rather than assuming the buyer already knows them.

Do I have to clean up before talking to David?

No renovation is needed to begin a discussion. Describe the condition and access honestly. Do not enter unsafe areas or dispose of records merely to make the property look ready.

Can I consider other buyers or an agent listing?

Yes. Compare actual written terms and your own priorities. You are not obligated to accept an offer because you called or filled out the inquiry form.

How do I confirm the operating company?

The About page identifies Washington Home Solutions LLC, UBI 606259805, and links to the official Washington business registry. Also confirm the purchasing party named in your actual agreement.

Is a closing date guaranteed when I first call?

No. A proposed date depends on the written agreement, title, ownership documents, payoffs and other remaining conditions. Ask which items are still unresolved before planning a move.

Still have a property question?

Inquiries go to Washington Home Solutions LLC. A property review is not an offer or a promise to purchase.
